Frequently asked questions

Can I book only a flight ticket?

Yes. Search domestic or international flights at the top of this page, or send us your route and dates and we'll share the best available fares.

Do you help with visas?

Yes, we assist with documentation and applications. Requirements differ by country, so we confirm exactly what you need before you apply.

Do you plan international holidays?

Yes. We plan holidays across India and abroad — flights, stays, transfers and visa help together in one plan.

Can you arrange group or corporate travel?

Yes. Tell us your group size, dates and destination and we'll plan flights, stays and transfers for everyone.

Are Sannidhi prices per person?

Prices are per day for a couple. Your final quote depends on the plan, number of days, travel dates and availability.

Is Guruvayur darshan assistance included?

Yes. Every Sannidhi plan — Signature, Sacred and Elite — includes Guruvayur darshan assistance.

What comes with every Sannidhi journey?

Airport pickup and drop, sightseeing and important place visits, food and dining, 24/7 assistance, special care for senior citizens and personalised experiences.

Can you plan a trip for elderly parents?

Yes. Special care for senior citizens is part of every Sannidhi journey, and we can plan any holiday around their pace.
